About Me

My educational journey is longer than others, but it has given me the chance to truly explore my passion for Computer Science. It started with hardware and robotics during my Higher NITEC in Mechatronics at ITE College West, which eventually evolved into my Diploma in Information Technology at Singapore Polytechnic. During my diploma, we were offered three different specializations, I chose the Immersive Simulation path, which sparked my passion for game design and development through creating VR games with Unity.

Currently, I am a Year 3 Computer Science in Interactive Media & Game Development (IMGD) student at SIT-DigiPen. My focus has shifted to the fascinating intersection of data, psychology, and game systems. In our first year, we built games from scratch using the school's custom engines and C/C++ programming. During our second year, we collaborated with students from other DigiPen courses to design and develop a game, and we also learned how to create our own custom game engine.

When I'm not looking at data or game engines, I believe in having a good work-life balance! You can usually find me at gyms, traveling (I love visiting family in China!), or unwinding by watching the latest anime and K-dramas. Some of my hobbies include drawing, reading and gaming during my free time too!
