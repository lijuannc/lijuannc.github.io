About Me

My technical journey is anything but traditional, and it has given me a unique perspective on how systems and humans interact. It started with hardware and robotics during my Higher Nitec in Mechatronics at ITE, which evolved into software engineering during my Diploma in Information Technology at Singapore Polytechnic.

Today, I am a Year 3 student in the Interactive Media & Game Development (IMGD) program at SIT-DigiPen. My focus has shifted to the fascinating intersection of data, psychology, and game systems. From conducting UX research and analyzing gameplay loops, to managing data solutions, I love using data to understand behavior and improve digital systems.

When I'm not looking at data or game engines: I believe in having a good work-life balance! You can usually find me playing badminton with friends, traveling (I love visiting family in China!), or unwinding by watching the latest anime and K-dramas.
